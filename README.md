## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **AI researcher and Director of an AI Innovation Lab**, specialising in **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **multi-agent workflows**, and **multimodal AI** applied to healthcare, education, and the public sector.

My work spans end-to-end system design — from embedding pipelines and vector stores through to agentic reasoning and evaluation frameworks — with a focus on **deployable, real-world AI** in multilingual and resource-constrained environments.

I deliver **NVIDIA DLI workshops**, advise on applied AI pilots, and lead student research projects at the intersection of NLP, computer vision, and agentic systems.

## 📍 Selected Production Work

- **Bilingual RAG benchmark (English + Uzbek)** — improved Uzbek retrieval recall from 39% to 98% via corpus engineering. Cohen's d = 2.91. Effect size 7.9x larger than model-swap optimisation. → [soas-rag-evaluation](https://github.com/rajantripathi/soas-rag-evaluation)
- **DialogXR (Bikal Technologies, UK)** — LangGraph multi-agent orchestration, Llama 3.1 8B via Ollama, fully air-gapped sovereign deployment on Lenovo ThinkSystem SR630 V4. Lenovo Validated Design LP2373.
- **Multimodal cancer prognosis** — CONCH V+C+G cross-attention; C-index 0.609, Stage 1 AUROC 0.741 (95% CI 0.649–0.825), log-rank p = 0.005. → [Breast-Cancer-Multimodal-AI](https://github.com/rajantripathi/Breast-Cancer-Multimodal-AI)
- **NVIDIA DLI Certified Instructor** — "Building RAG Agents with LLMs" and "Building AI Agents with Multimodal Models". Delivered to academic and enterprise engineering teams (EPAM, AUT).
